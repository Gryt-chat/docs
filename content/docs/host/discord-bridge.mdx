If your community lives on Discord, Gryt can copy it over and keep both sides in step while people move. It copies every channel with its whole history, files, threads, reactions, pins, roles and custom emoji. After that, new messages keep flowing, one way or both.

It's done by a Discord bot that you make yourself, in your own Discord account. It runs inside your Gryt server, so there's nothing extra to host. One Gryt server links to one Discord server.

Only the server's owner can set it up, because the bridge writes messages in other people's names.

## Setting it up

1. Open **Server settings → Discord**. On a new server with no messages yet, the first channel also offers it.
2. In the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications), make a new application. Call it what the bot should be called.
3. Under **Bot**, turn on **Message Content Intent**. Without it the bot can't read messages.
4. Copy the application ID and the bot token into the Discord tab and press **Connect**. Gryt checks the token with Discord before it saves it. The token stays on your server and never reaches anyone's app.
5. Press **Open invite link** and add the bot to your Discord server. You need Manage Server there. The bot only asks for what it uses: reading and sending messages, threads, webhooks, managing messages, reactions, files and links. It doesn't ask for Administrator.
6. Pick which way it goes, then press **Start copying**.

| Direction | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Discord → Gryt | Everything is copied here, and new Discord messages keep coming. Discord stays the main place. |
| Both ways | Same channels and messages on both sides. What happens on one shows up on the other. |
| Gryt → Discord | What's said here is posted into Discord, for people who haven't moved yet. |

A big server takes a while. Discord hands a bot 100 messages at a time and slows it down past that. The copy runs on the server, so you can close the app. If it stops partway, starting it again picks up where it left off.

## What comes across

- **Channels and categories.** Categories become folders. Voice channels come over too, but the voice itself isn't bridged.
- **Messages**, with replies, edits, reactions, pins and public threads. Forum posts become topics in a forum channel.
- **Files, pictures and avatars.** They're downloaded while the history is read and stored on your server, so nothing points back to Discord. A file over your server's upload limit is skipped, and its message says so.
- **Custom emoji**, as long as the name isn't taken here.
- **Roles**, with their names and colours. They rank between Member and Moderator, so an imported role can't act on your own moderators. Discord's Administrator gets everything except managing roles, the server, identities and bots.

Nothing is ever copied twice. Every copied message keeps its Discord id, so running the copy again, linking again later or using a new bot app only adds what's missing.

## When a server already has channels

The tab suggests which Discord channel goes where, matching names loosely, so "💬・general-chat" lands in #general. Check the suggestions and change any that are wrong. Each Discord channel can go to a channel that's already here, to a new one, or nowhere. Messages that are already here stay. Discord's history fits in around them by date.

## People

Someone who wrote on Discord shows up with their Discord name and picture until they're linked to a member here. There are two ways to link them:

- **They prove it themselves.** They run `/link` on the bot in Discord and get a code that only they can see. They enter it in Gryt, and their old Discord messages show under their account.
- **You link them by hand** in the Discord tab.

Both go in the audit log. You can always undo a link: the messages keep their Discord author underneath, so they go back to showing the Discord name.

A linked member also gets the roles they hold on Discord.

## Both ways

When the bridge goes both ways or Gryt → Discord, what members post here goes to Discord under their name. A few things come out differently there:

- **Replies** start with a link to the message they answer. Discord doesn't let a bot make a real reply under someone else's name.
- **Reactions** show as one from the bot per emoji, because Discord counts one per account. Custom Gryt emoji aren't sent.
- **Pictures on profiles** don't come across yet.

Deleting a message removes it on both sides.

## When something goes wrong

The Discord tab shows what's wrong and how to fix it:

- **The token stopped working**, because the app was deleted or the token was reset. Paste a new one.
- **Message Content Intent is off.** Turn it on in the Developer Portal and press Reconnect.
- **The bot was removed** from the Discord server. Add it back with the invite link.

While the bridge is down, nothing is lost. Each channel catches up from the last message it copied once the bot is back.

## Unlinking

**Unlink** in the Discord tab stops the bridge and logs the bot out. Everything already copied stays on your server, and so do member links. Linking the same Discord server again later picks up where it stopped.

## Your duties as the bot's owner

<Callout type="warn">
The Discord app is yours, so Discord's [Developer Terms](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383-Discord-Developer-Terms-of-Service) and [Developer Policy](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy) apply to you as its owner.
</Callout>

- **Have a privacy policy** for the app, linked in the Developer Portal. It should say what's copied and how someone can ask for it to be deleted.
- **Delete on request.** Anyone can run `/forget-me` on the bot to remove everything they wrote from your Gryt server. New messages they post on Discord are still copied while the bridge runs.
- **Tell your Discord server** that it's being mirrored, for example in its rules channel.
- **Keep the data safe.** Discord asks for data at rest to be encrypted. Gryt doesn't encrypt its database itself, so use disk encryption on the machine.
