## Voice

Gryt cleans up your audio before any of it leaves your machine: noise
suppression, a gate, auto gain. [Voice](/docs/use/voice) is the controls you use
day to day. [Audio processing](/docs/use/audio) says what each setting does to
the sound, and when to leave it alone.

## On a server

What you can do on a server depends on the roles you've been given there, and
whoever runs it decides those. [Roles and permissions](/docs/use/roles) goes
through the switches one by one. Adding your own emojis is in [Custom
emojis](/docs/use/emojis).

## If something is broken

[Troubleshooting](/docs/use/troubleshooting) first, then the
[FAQ](/docs/use/faq). If neither helps, the bug report form is in the app under
Settings.
